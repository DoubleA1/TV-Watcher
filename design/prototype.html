The actual product

Everything else is scaffolding around these four messages.

It says what it costs you

“Included with your subscription” is doing real work. A title being technically available on a service you have is not the same as being watchable tonight without paying again.

It fires at the unlock, not the next morning

Premiere times are published in advance, so the message is scheduled rather than discovered. The countdown on the welcome screen is the same clock that sends it.

A sequel getting announced is worth a text

Months before a date exists, somebody green-lit the thing you have been waiting for. Nobody else tells you this.

Nothing arrives at 3am unless you said so

Quiet hours hold overnight alerts until morning. A midnight drop on the west coast is 3am on the east, and a text that wakes you up is a text you turn off.

Not yet real

Sending these requires carrier registration (A2P 10DLC) that takes about a week to approve. Until it clears, alerts go by email and the phone path stays dark.
